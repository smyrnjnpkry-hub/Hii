You are a product engineer. Build a Whole-Life Productivity OS for a mobile-first app (390px). Productivity is not “more work.” It is reliable follow-through in every life domain without burning sleep or relationships.

SCIENCE / PRINCIPLES YOU MUST IMPLEMENT
- Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan): lasting drive needs autonomy, competence, relatedness. External shame streaks backfire.
- Energy regulation precedes time management (TMT research: energy is a top self-regulatory skill). If sleep < 6.5h, automatically shrink today’s deep-work load.
- Implementation intentions: every MIT gets a START CUE (after X, at place Y), not only a due date.
- Carry-forward: unfinished work stays visible tomorrow as “still open” with original cue noted. Never silently delete. (Open loops tax attention — Zeigarnik.)
- Temptation bundling and 2-minute starts from companion modules may be referenced but this module owns the week.
- Parkinson + attention: hard cap 3 MITs per day across ALL domains. Everything else is a parking lot, not a guilt list.
- Weekly review beats daily overplanning (12 minutes).
- Relatedness: optional one accountability human, not a public leaderboard.

DOMAINS (equal dignity, always visible)
1. Sleep / Body
2. Craft / Work
3. Mind / Learning
4. People
5. Money / Admin
6. Play / Recovery

Each domain has:
- 1 weekly outcome (observable)
- 1 daily tiny act
- 1 kill criterion (“I will not ___ this week”)
- a color-neutral icon, no gamified ranks

DAILY
- Morning 60s: show 3 MITs max, each with start cue and domain.
- Time remaining shown in hours.
- Drag order = commitment order. First MIT is the one that most needs a Start Engine.
- If sleep logged < 6.5h: banner “Load cut. Keep 1 MIT. Protect a 20-min walk and earlier lights-out.”
- Complete / skip / split. Skip asks for the emotion (feeds anti-procrastination), not a lecture.

WEEKLY REVIEW (12 min wizard)
1. Which cues fired?
2. Where did emotion stall?
3. One friction to add (bad) or remove (good).
4. One identity vote to keep.
5. Pick next week’s 6 domain outcomes, still 3 MITs/day cap.

CALENDAR
- Assign MITs to days. Incomplete carry forward with carriedFrom date.
- Recurring admin (bills, laundry, calls) live in Money/Admin or People, not Work.
- No infinite Google-Calendar clone. This is assignment of the 3 MITs + domain tiny acts.

DATA

Local persist.

SUCCESS METRICS
- MIT start rate (not hours)
- Domain coverage (did People and Recovery get a vote?)
- Sleep consistency
- Carry-forward age (old loops should shrink)

TONE
