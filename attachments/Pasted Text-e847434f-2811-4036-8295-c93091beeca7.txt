You are a product engineer and learning scientist. Build a Skill Acquisition Studio for a mobile-first app (390px). Naive repetition creates the OK plateau. Implement Ericsson deliberate practice, Fitts & Posner stages, Anderson ACT compilation, and Bjork desirable difficulties.

SCIENCE YOU MUST IMPLEMENT
- Deliberate practice (Ericsson & Pool): specific goal, full attention, immediate feedback, work at the edge of ability, preferably with a rubric/coach/recording. Comfortable reps are maintenance, not growth.
- Fitts & Posner:
  1) Cognitive — slow, verbal, error-full; need a clear mental model.
  2) Associative — smoother, fewer errors, less self-talk.
  3) Autonomous — automatic; improvement DIES unless you break automaticity on purpose.
- Desirable difficulties (Bjork): they feel worse and stick better:
  Spacing > cramming
  Interleaving (mix sub-skills) > blocked drills
  Retrieval practice > rereading
  Generate-then-check
- Interleaving trains discrimination (which method for this case).
- Hours logged ≠ skill. Progress = better score on a FIXED test.
- Unlearning old technique is required when switching methods (link conceptually to a replacement response; do not build the full HRT module here).

ONBOARDING
- Name one skill and a 12-week “good enough” definition that is observable (“hold a 3-minute conversation in Spanish about my work,” not “get good at Spanish”).
- Pick sub-skills (3–7).
- Self-diagnose stage per sub-skill with examples.
- Define a 10-minute fixed test you will retake every 14 days.

PRACTICE CARD (the unit)
- Target for THIS session (one sentence, harder than last time)
- Duration 25–50 min focused (or 12 min if energy is low — still at the edge)
- Difficulty: must be slightly uncomfortable. If user tags “easy,” app FORCES a harder variation next time
- Feedback: record / rubric 1–5 / note “what failed”
- End with retrieval: do it from memory, then check
- Log one change for next session

SCHEDULER
- Space sessions (min 1 rest day for the same sub-skill unless user overrides)
- Interleave 2–3 sub-skills per week, not 7 days of only A then 7 of B
- Plateau detector: if time-on-task high and difficulty/test flat for 3 sessions, show “OK plateau” and demand a harder drill or a coach/source

STAGE-SPECIFIC DEFAULTS
- Cognitive: watch/read a model, imitate slowly, error log, no speed
- Associative: mix contexts, reduce notes, time-box
- Autonomous: add constraints, speed, noise, or teach-back to break cruise control

DATA
Skill, Subskill { stage, test, scores[] }, Session { target, minutes, difficulty, feedback, nextChange }, PlateauFlags.
Local persist.

TONE
Demanding but fair. Honor struggle. Never “10,000 hours” as a progress bar.

SHIP
Skill setup, stage diagnosis, practice card, 14-day test reminder, interleaving week view, plateau warning, one demo skill (e.g. conversational English or guitar first riff) so the UI is alive.
