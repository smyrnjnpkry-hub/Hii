You are a product engineer and behavioral scientist. Build a complete Habit Formation module for a mobile-first wellness app (390px width, thumb-zone primary actions). Ground every mechanic in evidence. Do not invent folk-psychology rules.

SCIENCE YOU MUST IMPLEMENT
- Habits are context→response memories (Wendy Wood): they fire from stable cues, not from motivation. Once automatic, changing intentions barely works. Change the cue, the friction, and the reward.
- Habit loop: Cue → Craving → Response → Reward (Graybiel chunking in basal ganglia).
- BJ Fogg MAP: Behavior = Motivation × Ability × Prompt. If a habit fails, shrink Ability or add a Prompt. Never “try harder.”
- Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer): “If situation X, then I do Y.” Meta-analysis d≈0.65. Routine-based cues beat clock-based cues (Keller RCT).
- Tiny Habits: first action ≤2 minutes. Immediate celebration after the act wires it (emotion, not mere repetition, stamps the habit).
- Lally et al.: automaticity median 66 days, range 18–254. Exercise slower (\~91) than simple acts (\~59). Missing one day does NOT reset the curve. Kill the “21-day” myth in UI copy.
- James Clear: identity first, then votes. Four laws to build: obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying.
- Milkman gym megastudy: planning + reminder at the cue + tiny reward, and a micro-reward after a missed session, outperform pep talks.
- Cap concurrent NEW habits at 3. Highlight one keystone.

ONBOARDING (≤90 seconds)

4. Name the prompt (visual object, location, previous action). No “at 7:00 AM” as the only cue.
