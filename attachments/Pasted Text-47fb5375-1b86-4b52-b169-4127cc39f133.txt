You are a senior game designer + product designer + ACT/CBT clinician.

Take the “Reset Path” recovery app (porn + compulsive masturbation habit change) and GAMIFY THE ENTIRE PROCESS without making it shame-based, casino-like, or streak-hostage.

GOAL
Make recovery feel like a single-player RPG with optional co-op: the user levels up by practicing skills (urge surfing, values action, environment design), not by being “pure.” Abstinence is one quest line, not the only scoreboard.

NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN LAWS
1. Reward BEHAVIOR and SKILL, not moral purity.
   - Highest XP: completed urge-surf, logged a trigger honestly, did a replacement action, journaled a values move, locked the environment, helped a teammate.
   - Medium XP: clean day, daily check-in, lesson finished.
   - Lowest / zero XP: just opening the app.
2. Never punish a slip with identity damage.
   - A slip does NOT delete levels, titles, tree growth, or total XP.
   - A slip starts a Recovery Quest, not a “you failed” screen.
   - Never show “0 days” as the hero number. Always show Total Clean Days + Skills Mastered + Current Chapter.
3. Streaks are capped and recoverable.
   - Display a 7-day Cycle, not an infinite anxiety streak.
   - Completing a 7-day cycle stamps a Week Seal. Counter resets to Day 1 of next cycle. History of seals is the real record.
   - Streak Shields: earned (never bought). 1 shield every 7 completed cycles or after 5 successful urge-surfs. Using a shield keeps the cycle intact after a miss.
   - Grace window: if they check in within 18 hours of the usual time, cycle continues.
4. No pay-to-win, no monetized guilt, no “buy a streak freeze.”
5. Optional social only. Default is private. Leaderboards never rank “days clean.” They can rank “quests completed this week” or “urge-surfs completed” if the user opts in.
6. Tone: competent coach + game master. Adult, dry-warm, no “warrior purity,” no religious framing unless the user chooses a theme pack.
7. Dopamine design must compete with porn WITHOUT becoming another compulsive loop. Variable rewards are small and skill-tied. No loot-box spinning, no infinite scroll feed.

CORE METAPHOR
“Rewire” — the player is restoring a living system (neural garden / signal tower / inner city), not collecting chastity points.

Visual: a living map that grows as skills are practiced.
- Fog of war lifts as they complete education modules.
- Paths light up when they surf urges.
- A garden / tree / city district grows from ACTIONS, not from an unbroken streak. A slip wilts one leaf for 24 hours, then recovers if they do the Recovery Quest. The tree never dies.

PLAYER FANTASY
“I am becoming someone who can feel an urge and still choose my life.”

PLAYER LOOP (daily, 3–8 minutes unless SOS)
1. Morning Briefing (30–60s): today’s 3 Daily Quests + one optional Side Quest.
2. Live the day.
3. If urge hits → SOS mini-game (Urge Surf).
4. Evening Debrief: check-in, loot XP, see map grow, next unlock tease.

PROGRESSION SYSTEMS

A. XP & Levels (always accumulating)
Sources of XP (tune numbers):
- Daily check-in: 10
- Finish today’s lesson (3–8 min): 25
- Complete a replacement action: 20
- Honest urge log (acted or not): 15
- Full Urge Surf protocol completed: 40
- Environment lock (blocker on, phone out of bedroom, night lock): 15
- Values action (text a friend, gym, walk, create): 30
- Recovery Quest after slip: 50 (must be the highest single reward so restart feels like a win)
- 7-day Cycle seal: 100
Level titles tied to SKILL, not days:
  L1 Spark → L2 Cartographer (mapped triggers) → L3 Surfer → L4 Architect (environment) → L5 Compass (values) → L6 Steward → L7 Signal → L8 Unhooked
Unlock cosmetics and tools by level, not by perfect abstinence.

B. Three parallel meters (Apple Rings style, but kinder)
1. Presence — check-ins, journaling, lessons
2. Mastery — urge-surfs, defusion drills, CBT thought records
3. Life — exercise, sleep, social, creative, time outdoors
Closing all 3 rings stamps the day. Missing one ring does not zero the others.

C. Quests
Daily Quests (always 3, one from each meter). Examples:
- “Surf or delay one urge for 10 minutes”
- “Write 2 sentences: what was I actually needing?”
- “10 minutes walk or 20 bodyweight reps”
- “Phone charges outside bedroom tonight”
Weekly Boss Quests (choose 1):
- Map Top 3 triggers from 14 days of logs
- 3 social contacts
- 4 workouts
- Full environment lockdown audit
Chapter Quests (story):
  Chapter 1 The Map (days 1–7): education + trigger cartography
  Chapter 2 The Wave (days 8–21): urge surfing mastery
  Chapter 3 The Room (days 22–35): stimulus control
  Chapter 4 The Compass (days 36–56): ACT values + committed action
  Chapter 5 The City (days 57–90): identity + relapse prevention
  Epilogue Open World: maintenance, seasonal events, skill trees
Each chapter ends with a short “boss”: a synthesized high-risk scenario they plan for in-app.

D. Recovery Quest (the most important game design)
On slip:
- No red FAIL splash. Screen title: “The wave hit. Time to rebuild the levee.”
- 3-step quest (10–15 min):
  1. Debrief: when, where, emotion, 10 minutes before (HALT + trigger)
  2. One system change (move charger, add night lock, delete an account, schedule morning workout)
  3. One values action in the next 2 hours
- Completing it grants a large XP bonus + a “Kintsugi” badge (gold seam on the map).
- Current 7-day cycle may break; Week Seals, level, tree, and total clean days stay.
- Offer a Shield if they have one. Using it is framed as strategy, not cheating.

E. Urge Surf = the combat system
This is the game’s real-time encounter.
Flow:
1. Panic / Encounter button
2. Name the enemy: “Urge — intensity X/10 — source: boredom / night / loneliness…”
3. 90-second grounding (breath + body scan) — a “stance” mini-game, not a cartoon fight
4. Ride the wave: intensity slider every 60s for 8–12 min, visualized as a wave they stay on top of
5. Choose a Finisher: leave room / cold water / 20 reps / text ally / values reminder
6. Loot: XP + a drop (insight card: “Night + isolation is your elite mob”)
Never show porn, never use sexy imagery, never use the user’s camera to shame their face.

F. Inventory (earned, not purchased)
- Streak Shields
- Night Lantern (auto reminder at user’s high-risk hour)
- Blueprint cards (environment changes)
- Companion phrases (user-written “why” lines that appear mid-surf)
- Theme packs: Monastery, Cyberpunk City, Forest, Quiet Lab (visual only)

G. Optional co-op (off by default)
- Party of 2–5. Shared weekly boss: “collect 20 Mastery points as a team.”
- Help tokens: send a “surf with me” ping. No public slip feed.
- No ranking of abstinence. If a board exists, it is “quests finished” or “rings closed.”
- Clans by interest (gym, students, partners, night-shift) not by ideology unless user opts in.

H. Anti-shame UX details
- Hero stats on home: Level, Week Seals, Total Clean Days, Urges Surfed, Recovery Quests completed.
- Current cycle shown as 7 dots, not “Day 47 or die.”
- After day 60 of stable practice, graduate the user: daily streak pressure drops; weekly seals + seasonal challenges remain. Habit is becoming identity.
- Notifications: “Your high-risk window starts in 20 minutes. Lantern is on.” Never “Don’t you dare.”

NARRATIVE & COPY RULES
- You are the Game Master of their nervous system, not a priest.
- Copy examples:
  Slip: “Data acquired. The map just got more accurate.”
  Surf success: “You stayed on the board. The wave is smaller than it felt.”
  Week seal: “Seven days of choosing. Stamp earned.”
- Ban: “You’ve ruined it,” “addict,” “gooner,” “defeat,” countdown-to-zero shame, sexualized avatars, loot boxes.

CONTENT THAT GETS GAMIFIED (keep the clinical spine)
Wrap the original 90-day CBT/ACT curriculum as Chapters and side-quests:
- Psychoeducation → lore drops
- Trigger log → cartography skill tree
- Cognitive defusion → “Untangle” mini-game (label thought vs fact)
- Values → Compass relic they rewrite monthly
- Stimulus control → Architect skill
- Relapse prevention → City walls
Every lesson ends with a 1-tap quest, not a lecture only.

ANALYTICS THE GAME SHOULD USE
After 14 logs, spawn a “Boss Intel” card:
“80% of encounters happen 22:00–01:00, alone, phone in bed, after scrolling. Recommended quest: Night Lock + charger exile.”
This card IS the loot. Make pattern-insight feel like finding the enemy’s weakness.

TECH / PRODUCT NOTES FOR BUILDERS
- Home screen: map fragment + 3 rings + 3 daily quests + giant SOS button.
- Widget: SOS + today’s cycle dots.
- Local-first progress so a missed server doesn’t break the game.
- Accessibility: low-stimulation mode that strips particles but keeps XP and quests.
- Monetization: full game loop free through Chapter 2. Subscribe for Chapters 3–5, AI GM, party, extra themes. Never sell Shields.

DELIVERABLES
Produce:
1. Game design document (core loop, economy, progression, Recovery Quest, Urge combat).
2. Home, SOS, slip, and week-seal screen copy.
3. XP table and level curve for 90 days.
4. 14-day onboarding quest list (day-by-day).
5. Badge/title list with unlock rules (skill-based).
6. What we explicitly will NOT gamify (shame, camera humiliation, abstinence leaderboards, paid streak saves).
7. Edge cases: user wants harm reduction not full abstinence; user has a partner; user is in a flatline; user binge-slips 3 days.

Design this so a person at 1 a.m. opens it because the encounter system helps them survive the next 12 minutes — not because they are terrified of losing Day 63.
